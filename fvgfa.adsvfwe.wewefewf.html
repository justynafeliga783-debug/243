Your Privacy Matters

Nova is designed with a privacy-first approach. This notice explains how the application operates and what you can expect when using it. Please read it carefully before proceeding.

Nova does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The application runs entirely on your device and requires no network connection for its core functionality.

What We Do Not Do

Data Handling

Any preferences or settings you configure within Nova are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded, synchronized, or shared. Uninstalling the application removes all locally stored data permanently.

Third-Party Services
